# Installation & License Activation

This guide walks you through installing Nexora on your WordPress website and activating your software license for automatic updates.

---

## Step 1: Download the Plugin ZIP

1. Log in to your [CodeClove Account](https://codeclove.com/account/).
2. Navigate to the **Downloads** tab.
3. Click **Download** next to **Nexora - Education Management System** to obtain the `nexora.zip` archive.
4. Copy your **License Key** displayed on the same page.

::: tip Keep your License Key handy
Your license key is also included in your purchase confirmation email.
:::

---

## Step 2: Upload to WordPress

1. Log in to your WordPress admin dashboard (`/wp-admin`).
2. Go to **Plugins > Add New Plugin**.
3. Click the **Upload Plugin** button at the top.
4. Click **Choose File** and select the downloaded `nexora.zip` file.
5. Click **Install Now**.
6. Once the installation completes, click **Activate Plugin**.

---

## Step 3: Activate Your License Key

Activating your license key enables one-click automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and validates priority support access.

1. In your WordPress dashboard, navigate to **Nexora > Settings > License**.
2. Paste your license key into the **License Key** field.
3. Click **Activate License**.
4. The status badge will update to **Active**.

::: info Staging & Local Environments
Your license key can be activated on local development environments (`.test`, `.local`, `localhost`) and staging subdomains (`staging.*`) without counting against your live site activation limits.
:::

---
