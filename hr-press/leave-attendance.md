# Leave & Attendance

Manage annual leave allocations, approval workflows, and daily attendance tracking.

---

## 1. Creating Leave Types & Quotas

1. Go to **HR Press > Leave > Leave Types**.
2. Click **Add Leave Type**.
3. Configure policy parameters:
   * **Name:** `Paid Time Off (PTO)`, `Sick Leave`, `Casual Leave`, etc.
   * **Days Allowed:** Set total annual quota (e.g., `18 days/year`).
   * **Carry Forward:** Allow unused balance to roll over into the next calendar year.
   * **Requires Approval:** Set whether requests require manager or admin sign-off.
4. Click **Save Policy**.

---

## 2. Managing Leave Requests

When an employee submits a leave request through the self-service dashboard:

1. Managers/Admins receive an email notification.
2. Go to **HR Press > Leave > Requests**.
3. View the employee's request, duration, reason, and remaining balance.
4. Click **Approve** or **Reject** with optional feedback notes.
5. The employee's leave balance is automatically adjusted.

---

## 3. Daily Attendance & Clock-in

HR Press Pro includes self-service clock-in for team members:

1. **Web Dashboard Clock-in:** Employees click **Clock In** on their dashboard upon starting work.
2. **Attendance Log:** Navigate to **HR Press > Attendance > Logs** to view daily check-in times, check-out times, and total work hours.
3. **Monthly Timesheets:** Export monthly attendance summaries for payroll processing via CSV/Excel.
