# Payroll & Payslips

Configure employee salary structures, allowances, deductions, and generate printable monthly payslips.

---

## 1. Setting Up Employee Salary Profiles

1. Navigate to **HR Press > Payroll > Salary Setup** (or open an employee's profile in **HR Press > Employees**).
2. Select the employee and configure their compensation parameters:
   * **Salary Basis:** `Monthly Fixed`, `Hourly Rate`, or `Daily Rate`.
   * **Basic Salary:** Base compensation amount.
   * **Allowances (Earnings):** e.g., House Rent Allowance (HRA), Transport Allowance, Medical Allowance, Performance Bonus.
   * **Deductions:** e.g., Tax Withholding, Provident Fund (PF), Health Insurance, Advance Loan Repayment.
   * **Net Calculated Salary:** Basic Salary + Total Allowances - Total Deductions.
3. Click **Save Salary Structure**.

---

## 2. Generating Monthly Payslips

At the end of each billing cycle:

1. Navigate to **HR Press > Payroll > Generate Payslip**.
2. Select the **Pay Month** (e.g., `September 2026`) and optional **Department**.
3. Choose **Generate Mode**:
   * **Single Employee:** Generate payslip for a specific staff member.
   * **Bulk Generation:** Automatically calculates and generates payslips for all active employees across selected departments.
4. The system calculates:
   * Gross Salary
   * Unpaid Leave / Absence Deductions (linked directly to attendance logs)
   * Net Payable Amount
5. Click **Confirm & Generate Payslips**.

---

## 3. Viewing, Printing, and Exporting Payslips

* **Admin View:** Navigate to **HR Press > Payroll > Payslips** to view payment status (`Paid`, `Pending`, `Draft`).
* **Printable PDF:** Click **Print Payslip** to open a print-optimized payslip formatted with company logo, employee details, earnings breakdown, deductions, and authorized signature lines.
* **Employee Self-Service:** Employees can view and download their past monthly payslips directly from the front-end dashboard using `[hrp_dashboard]`.
