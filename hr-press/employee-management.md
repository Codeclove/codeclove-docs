# Employee Directory

Manage employee profiles, employment terms, emergency contacts, identity documents, and linked WordPress accounts.

---

## 1. Adding a New Employee

1. Navigate to **HR Press > Employees**.
2. Click **Add New Employee**.
3. Complete the employee profile sections:

### Personal Information
* **First Name & Last Name:** Full legal name.
* **Email Address:** Work email (used for notifications and login credentials).
* **Phone Number:** Primary contact number.
* **Date of Birth & Gender:** Standard demographic records.
* **Current & Permanent Address:** Residential contact info.
* **Profile Photo:** Upload avatar image.

### Employment Details
* **Employee ID:** e.g., `EMP-001`, `EMP-042` (custom identifier sequence).
* **Department:** Select from your configured departments.
* **Designation:** Select from designations within the chosen department.
* **Date of Joining:** Official contract start date.
* **Employment Type:** `Full-Time`, `Part-Time`, `Contractor`, `Intern`.
* **Shift:** Assign the default work schedule.
* **Status:** `Active`, `Inactive`, `Terminated`, `On Leave`.

### WordPress User Account Linking
* **Link Existing User:** Select an existing WordPress user from the dropdown.
* **Create New User:** Automatically creates a WordPress login with the `Employee` role and sends welcome credentials via email.

### Emergency Contacts & Documents
* **Emergency Contact:** Name, relationship, and emergency phone number.
* **Document Attachments:** Upload employment contract, tax forms (W-4, Form 16), government ID proof, and academic certificates.

4. Click **Save Employee**.

---

## 2. Managing Employee Profiles

In **HR Press > Employees**, the master table displays all staff with search, department filters, and status tags:

* **View Profile:** View complete employee dossier including personal info, attendance history, leave balances, and salary slips.
* **Edit:** Update contact details, promote to a new designation, or change department.
* **Deactivate / Terminate:** Sets status to `Inactive` or `Terminated`. Inactive employees can no longer clock in or submit leave requests.

---

## 3. Bulk CSV Import / Export

1. Go to **HR Press > Employees > Import/Export**.
2. **Export:** Download full staff directory as CSV for payroll or tax reporting.
3. **Import:** Upload a formatted CSV file to onboard multiple employees at once.
