# Announcements

Broadcast company-wide announcements, policy updates, and department-specific notices.

---

## 1. Creating an Announcement

1. Navigate to **HR Press > Announcements**.
2. Click **Add Announcement**.
3. Configure the announcement fields:
   * **Title:** e.g., `Annual Company Retreat 2026`, `New Health Insurance Policy Details`.
   * **Announcement Content:** Rich text editor for body copy, instructions, and bullet points.
   * **Target Audience:**
     * `All Employees`: Broadcast to all registered staff members.
     * `Department Specific`: Target specific operational units (e.g., *Engineering Only*, *Sales Team Only*).
   * **Publish Date & Expiry Date:** Define when the notice appears and when it should be archived.
   * **Attachment:** Optional PDF or document download (e.g., updated employee handbook).
4. Click **Publish Announcement**.

---

## 2. Where Announcements Appear

* **Admin Dashboard:** Listed under the notices widget on the primary HR Press dashboard.
* **Front-End Employee Portal:** Displayed at the top of the employee dashboard (`[hrp_dashboard]`) with badge indicators for unread updates.
* **Email Broadcast:** If email notifications are enabled, publishing a notice automatically sends a summary email to all targeted employees.
