# Holiday Calendars

Manage company-wide non-working days, national holidays, and regional observances.

---

## 1. Adding a Holiday

1. Navigate to **HR Press > Holidays**.
2. Click **Add Holiday**.
3. Configure holiday details:
   * **Holiday Name:** e.g., `New Year's Day`, `Independence Day`, `Diwali`, `Christmas`.
   * **Start Date:** Calendar date when the holiday starts.
   * **End Date:** For multi-day holidays, select the final day. For single-day holidays, set same date as start date.
   * **Number of Days:** Auto-calculated.
   * **Recurring Holiday:** Toggle to automatically duplicate this holiday on the same date each year.
   * **Description:** Optional details regarding company policy during the holiday.
4. Click **Save Holiday**.

---

## 2. Holiday Calendar Display

* **Admin Calendar:** View upcoming holidays in **HR Press > Holidays** (list and monthly calendar view).
* **Employee Self-Service Portal:** Staff can view upcoming company holidays from the front-end dashboard using `[hrp_dashboard]` or the holiday widget.
* **Attendance Impact:** Days marked as holidays are automatically excluded from absence penalty calculations and do not deduct from employee leave balances.
