# Installation & License Activation

Install HR Press Pro, activate your license, and enable automatic dashboard updates.

---

::: tip Prerequisites
Ensure your server meets the minimum requirements: **PHP 7.0+**, **WordPress 5.5+**, and **MySQL 5.6+ / MariaDB 10.1+**.
:::

<div class="step-list">
  <div class="step-item" id="step-1-download-the-plugin-zip">
    <div class="step-number">1</div>
    <div class="step-title">Download the Plugin ZIP</div>
    <p>Log in to your <a href="https://codeclove.com/account/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CodeClove Account</a>, navigate to the <strong>Downloads</strong> section, and click <strong>Download</strong> next to <strong>HR Press Pro</strong> to get the <code>hr-press-pro.zip</code> archive. Copy your <strong>License Key</strong>.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="step-item" id="step-2-upload-and-activate-in-wordpress">
    <div class="step-number">2</div>
    <div class="step-title">Upload and Activate in WordPress</div>
    <ol>
      <li>Log in to your WordPress admin dashboard (<code>/wp-admin</code>).</li>
      <li>Go to <strong>Plugins &gt; Add New Plugin</strong>.</li>
      <li>Click <strong>Upload Plugin</strong> at the top.</li>
      <li>Select <code>hr-press-pro.zip</code> and click <strong>Install Now</strong>.</li>
      <li>Click <strong>Activate Plugin</strong>.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="step-item" id="step-3-activate-your-license-key">
    <div class="step-number">3</div>
    <div class="step-title">Activate Your License Key</div>
    <p>To enable in-dashboard automatic updates and priority support:</p>
    <ol>
      <li>In the admin menu, click <strong>HR Press &gt; Settings &gt; License</strong>.</li>
      <li>Paste your license key into the designated input field.</li>
      <li>Click <strong>Activate License</strong>.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</div>

::: info Staging Support
Your license key allows unlimited local staging activations (`*.test`, `*.local`, `localhost`, `staging.*`).
:::
