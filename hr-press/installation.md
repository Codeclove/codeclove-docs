# Installation & License Activation

This guide details how to install HR Press Pro on your WordPress site and activate your license for automatic updates.

---

## Step 1: Download the Plugin ZIP

1. Log in to your [CodeClove Account](https://codeclove.com/account/).
2. Navigate to the **Downloads** section.
3. Click **Download** next to **HR Press Pro** to get the `hr-press-pro.zip` file.
4. Copy your **License Key** from your purchase receipt.

---

## Step 2: Upload and Activate in WordPress

1. Log in to your WordPress admin dashboard (`/wp-admin`).
2. Navigate to **Plugins > Add New Plugin**.
3. Click **Upload Plugin** at the top.
4. Select `hr-press-pro.zip` and click **Install Now**.
5. After upload completes, click **Activate Plugin**.

---

## Step 3: Activate Your License Key

1. In the WordPress admin menu, click **HR Press > Settings > License**.
2. Paste your license key into the designated input field.
3. Click **Activate License**.
4. The status indicator will turn green with an **Active** status.

::: info Staging Support
Your license key allows unlimited local staging activations (`*.test`, `*.local`, `localhost`, `staging.*`).
:::
